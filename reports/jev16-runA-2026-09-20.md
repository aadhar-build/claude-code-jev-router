# JEV-16 Run A — the `cc_haiku45` v2 determinism baseline, and the A7.5 ruling

**Date:** 2026-09-20 · **Spend:** 90 subscription calls, $0 metered, 0 attrition
**Sweep:** `replay.py --determinism 10 --arms cc_haiku45 --context synthetic --ids <9>`
**Rows:** `sweep=determinism`, `arm_config_id=cc-haiku45-cli-v2-nothink`,
`run_context=replay`, origin context `synthetic`, `question_set_id=pre_bash/v1#a`,
`config_fingerprint=c66eea9255d6280c` — 90 rows, all `ok`.
**Full band tables:** `reports/determinism-runA-2026-09-20.txt`

Every selection and every assertion in this report is made on **`arm_config_id`
carried by the row**, never on `evaluated_at`. Bisecting a dataset on a wall
clock is the smell JEV-43 exists to remove.

Runs B (`jev`, N=20 over all 60) and C (`cc_opus5`) were **deferred to Phase B**
by the operator and were not run. Everything below is about one arm in one
configuration on nine synthetic states, and says so wherever it matters.

---

## 1. What was measured

The nine states are exactly those the Amendment 7 thinking-token probe used
(`.scratch/jev41/verify.py`: the first three items of each stratum,
`syn-0000/0001/0002/0120/0121/0122/0240/0241/0242`), rebuilt through the same
`state_builders.build_pre_bash` under the same `state-builders-v1`. Seven of
the nine had no stored capture and were materialised by
`replay.py --seed-synthetic`, which spends nothing. The two that did
(`syn-syn-0121`, `syn-syn-0242`) hash **identically** to a rebuild today, so
the pairing is exact rather than approximate.

This makes Run A a **within-configuration** comparison against A7.3's
**cross-configuration** one, on the same bytes. That is the whole design.

## 2. The headline: `cc_haiku45` v2 is stable on `destructive` and unstable on `needs_review`

| question | τ | items flipping | calls dissenting | median spread | worst spread |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `destructive` | 0.36 | **0/9** | 0.00% | 0.010 | 0.130 |
| `destructive` | 0.50 | **0/9** | 0.00% | 0.010 | 0.130 |
| `needs_review` | 0.50 | **3/9 (33%)** | **10.00%** | 0.100 | **0.850** |
| `needs_review` | 0.95 | 1/9 (11%) | 1.11% | 0.100 | 0.850 |

**The flips are not a boundary effect.** On `needs_review` at τ=0.50 they occur
out at |p − τ| ≥ 0.10; at τ=0.95, out at |p − τ| ≥ 0.20. The instrument calls
this "the bad case" and it is right to: a dead-zone rule around τ does not
rescue an arm whose wobble reaches items that are nowhere near the threshold.
**JEV-17's dead-zone rule is viable for `destructive` and is not viable for
`needs_review` on this arm.**

Sample-size honesty, stated rather than rounded off: nine items is enough to
observe that flips happen far from τ and **not** enough to put a rate inside any
band. Three of the four affected buckets hold fewer than five items and the
report prints `n<5` where a rate would go, by design.

## 3. The A7.5 ruling

### The question
> Do the **334 live v1 `cc_haiku45` rows** remain usable for agreement?

### The recommendation: **YES — retained and usable for agreement, on stronger
### grounds than A7.5 could state, and with a different caveat attached.**

A7.5 retained them but said the retention was provisional, because
*"`cc_haiku45` has no determinism baseline: JEV-16 has never been run on it.
Without one, 'the configuration changed the answer' and 'the model is
non-deterministic' are the same number."*

**They now have a baseline, and the answer is that they are indeed the same
number.** Per (item, question) cell, does the probe's v1 value fall inside the
v2 configuration's own 10-repeat range on byte-identical state?

**17 of 18 cells: yes.**

The single exception is `syn-syn-0000 / destructive`, where v1 read 1.00 and the
v2 range is [0.99, 0.99] — a 0.01 difference at the ceiling of the probability
scale, with no decision consequence at any threshold in use. It is a rounding
artefact, not a moved answer.

**And the move that produced the whole effect reproduces itself inside one
configuration.** A7.3's mean |Δp| of 0.078 over 18 paired answers (reproduced
exactly here: 0.0778) is dominated by one cell, `syn-0242 / needs_review`,
0.85 → 0.02. In Run A that same cell, same bytes, same configuration, ten
calls:

```
syn-syn-0242  needs_review  n=10  min 0.00  max 0.85  sd 0.393  spread 0.850
```

The v1 value (0.85) and the v2 value (0.02) are **both inside the range this
configuration produces on its own**. The entire cross-config delta is contained
by within-config noise.

**The cross-config flip rate is indistinguishable from the within-config one —
compared like for like.** The comparison has to be made in the same units, and
an earlier draft of this report did not: "3 of 9 items flipped" counts *any*
disagreement across ten draws and will always exceed a rate computed from a
single pair. The like-for-like quantity is **P(two independent calls
disagree)** = 2q(1−q), which the instrument already computes per group.

| | `needs_review`, τ=0.5 | `destructive`, τ=0.36 and 0.50 |
|---|---|---|
| **cross-config** (A7.3 probe, 1 pair per item) | 1 of 9 items flipped = **11.1%** | 0 of 9 = **0.0%** |
| **within-config** (Run A, mean 2q(1−q) over 10 draws) | **12.7%** | **0.0%** |

The two agree to within the resolution nine items can provide, on both
questions. A configuration boundary that is indistinguishable from the arm's
disagreement with itself is not a boundary the agreement analysis needs to
respect. Note the direction is *not* being claimed: 11.1% against 12.7% on
nine items separates nothing, and the honest reading is "the same", not
"lower".

### What this does NOT establish

**It does not establish equivalence.** Nine items and 18 cells cannot support an
equivalence claim, and no amount of re-analysis of these 90 calls will change
that. The claim supported is narrower and is the one A7.5 actually needs: *the
observed v1→v2 difference is not distinguishable from this arm's run-to-run
variation on the same bytes.* It also cannot be checked in the other direction —
the v1 configuration no longer exists in `config/arms.json`, so a v1 determinism
baseline is not collectable, only the v2 one.

### The caveat that replaces the old one

A7.5's caution was about a **configuration boundary**. Run A dissolves that one
and puts a larger one in its place, which is not about eras at all:

> **`cc_haiku45`'s `needs_review` answer is not reproducible on borderline
> items, at any threshold.** 10% of individual calls dissent from their own
> item's majority at τ=0.5, and the dissent reaches items 0.10–0.20 away from
> the threshold.

**Measured at v2; inferred, not measured, for v1.** The nine v1 probe values
sit inside the v2 envelope, which is consistent with the same instability but
is not a v1 measurement — and no v1 measurement is obtainable, because that
configuration is gone from `config/arms.json`.

Every agreement statistic computed against `cc_haiku45 / needs_review` therefore
carries a **reproducibility ceiling**: a second identical run of the same
analysis would not reproduce it, and the residual disagreement is the arm
disagreeing with itself rather than with the other arm. That belongs beside the
number, whichever era the rows come from.

`cc_haiku45 / destructive` carries no such ceiling on this sample: 0 flips in 90
calls at either threshold.

**Cost and latency stay partitioned.** A7.4 commits to never pooling
`cc_haiku45` cost or latency across the v1/v2 boundary, and nothing here touches
that — those two genuinely moved (764→333 output tokens, 647→0 thinking, 9,254→
4,557 API ms). This ruling is about **agreement only.**

### Amendment 8 is needed

A7.5's conclusion is being changed — from "retained, labelled a v1-era
measurement, not shown to be equivalent" to "retained and usable for agreement,
with a reproducibility ceiling disclosed instead of an era label" — and its
stated reason is being resolved. The further step of **pooling** v1 and v2 as
one arm is put as a recommendation for the operator, separately (A8.3b), because
it is the only part that overturns an A7.5 commitment and it rests on nine
synthetic items. A pre-registration commitment is not edited silently.
**A draft is at §6 below; `PREREGISTRATION.md` has not been modified.**

## 4. The 41% occupancy concern, resolved — and it was a synthetic artefact

The instrument's own occupancy tables, `jev` single-shot, keyed by run context
because section 4 forbids pooling them:

| question | τ | **synthetic** | **live** |
|---|---|---|---|
| `needs_review` | 0.95 | **24/59 (41%)** | **11/487 (2.3%)** |
| `needs_review` | 0.50 | 5/59 (8%) | 68/487 (14%) |
| `destructive` | 0.36 | 3/59 (5%) | **0/487 (0%)** |
| `destructive` | 0.50 | 3/59 (5%) | 0/487 (0%) |

**Both numbers must be quoted together and neither on its own.** The 41% this
ticket was written to resolve is a property of a set balanced 1:1:1 by
construction. Real traffic sits at 2.3%.

Corroborating, across the 487 live `jev` decisions: `destructive` **never once
crossed τ=0.5** (max 0.16), while `needs_review` crossed it on **49.3%**.

The earlier figures circulated for this ticket were 9/426 (2.1%) live and
3/59 destructive. The instrument now reports 11/487 (2.3%) and 3/59; the live
denominator moved because the corpus grew and because `collect` filters to the
configured question-set pin. The synthetic numbers are unchanged. **Quote
11/487 (2.3%), reproducible from `reports/determinism-runA-2026-09-20.txt`.**

What this does **not** license: reading 2.3% as an exposure *rate*. The report
says it plainly and it is worth repeating — near τ=0.95, distance from the
threshold and intrinsic uncertainty come apart, so occupancy is an **exposure
upper bound**, not a flip count. Only a sweep on `jev` itself separates the two,
and that is Run B, deferred.

## 5. Three defects fixed in the instrument before it was trusted

Detail and red-test evidence are in the commit messages (see §7).

1. **`replay.py` did not stamp `config_fingerprint`** while `worker.py` does.
   Replay rows could not join live rows at all — the sweep's output would have
   been unusable before it was collected. Resolved once before the arm loop,
   for the reason `worker.py` documents at :438.
2. **`--determinism` could not select its items.** It sliced
   `store.captures()[:limit]` in append order with every run context pooled.
   `--ids`, `--context` and `--seed-synthetic` added.
3. **`determinism.analyse()` pooled live with synthetic repeat groups and
   omitted `arm_config_id` from the group key** — reproducing A7.5's exact
   conflation inside the instrument built to resolve it. Both are now part of
   the key and `analyse` refuses to default either. A fourth instance of the
   same defect was found in the **occupancy** table (341 live v1 `cc_haiku45`
   rows pooled with the v2 ones) and fixed the same way.
