==============================================================================
DOES THE THRESHOLD SURVIVE A TRAIN/TEST SPLIT?
==============================================================================

A threshold fitted and scored on the same items is not a measurement, it is
a description. This tool refits it on half the DECISIONS and scores it on the
other half, many times over. The number to read is the OPTIMISM GAP: how much
of the published performance was the fit describing its own sample.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO READ THE GAP
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The gap is measured by fitting on HALF the items. The published threshold
  was fitted on all of them, so its true optimism is somewhat SMALLER than
  what is printed here -- more training data means less overfitting. The
  direction is what transfers: a gap of ~0 here means the published tau is
  safe, and a large gap here means it is not, but the magnitude is an upper
  bound, not a point estimate.

  With few positives per half, test TPR moves in coarse steps and the
  selected tau scatters. That scatter is the RESULT, not noise to be
  averaged away: it is how unstable the operating point genuinely is at
  this sample size.
