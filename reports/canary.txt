WHAT THIS CAN AND CANNOT TELL YOU
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * `response_model` catches a RENAMED model. It cannot catch a silently
    retrained one served under the same string -- that is what the
    probability deltas are for, and it is why they are reported per state
    rather than as a single mean.
  * One call per state per day. The `borderline` stratum sits near tau by
    design, and PREREGISTRATION section 7 records that Jev wobbles there.
    A flip inside the jitter band is consistent with ordinary run-to-run
    variance; a flip outside it is not. The tool prints which, and refuses
    to decide for you -- separating the two needs repeats, which is
    determinism.py's job, not this one's.
  * Canary rows carry run_context='canary' and are never pooled with live,
    replay or synthetic rows (PREREGISTRATION section 4).
